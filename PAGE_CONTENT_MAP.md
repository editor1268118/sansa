## Explicit editorial decisions
Original promotional claims and count-up statistics (3 years, 8 years, 8,000 patients, 6,000 procedures), “No.1”, guaranteed/pain-free results and unsupported aggregateRating 4.6/7774 are archived, not promoted as verified current facts. Doctor source biographies remain verbatim; their eight-year figure is not extrapolated. Free FibroScan third-Monday and free consultation offers are documented as historical and require confirmation. The laser FAQ's truncated Nd answer is preserved with an explicit request to discuss suitability with the specialist. No new treatment, credential, review, address or facility is invented.
